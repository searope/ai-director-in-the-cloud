Director

Turn a short brief into a finished film. Planned flow, built step by step:

  1. BriefYour idea, genre, length and style.
  2. Script & shot listWritten with your AI subscription (Claude Code editing the project's storyboard file) or a local model on the GPU, and editable before rendering.
  3. KeyframesOne or two keyframes per shot, kept consistent with your Characters.
  4. ShotsEach shot renders on its own. Regenerate, reorder or retime a shot without re-rendering the rest.
  5. FilmShots joined with hard cuts, then upscaling, voice-over and music.

Until then, use Create image for keyframes and Create video for each shot.

Characters

Named characters with reference images, tagged in shots and passed as references when making keyframes, so faces and outfits stay the same from shot to shot. Later: training one character LoRA on the GPU.

For now: make a character's first keyframe in Create image, then use Edit on it for each new pose.
